Rules in this folder are examples. Copy the ones you want into

    ~/Library/Application Support/notproton/rules/

and they apply at the next launch. The format is documented in
`10-launch-rules.conf`, and it is the same grammar as `examples/plugins`.

NotProton ships no per-game bypass flags of its own. A flag that helps one game
does nothing for the next and can stop a third from starting, so the ones that
work are collected here by the people who tested them rather than guessed at by
the program.
